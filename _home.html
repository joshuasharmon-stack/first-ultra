Built for bigger runners

Gentler build. More recovery. Same finish.

Most training plans assume a light runner. Turn on joint protection and your joints get the time to adapt that your heart and lungs don't need.

  • Longer walk breaks to start
  • A slower weekly build, about 6% instead of 10%
  • A lighter week every third week
  • One run a week swapped for low-impact cardio, like a bike or elliptical

Recommended above about 220 lb (100 kg), or with past shin, Achilles, foot or knee trouble.
